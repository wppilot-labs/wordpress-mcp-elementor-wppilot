# Ada Moreno Photography — wedding photographer landing page

Design and build a **complete, production-quality landing page** for "Ada Moreno Photography", a solo wedding photographer. **You own the design.** If web search is available, research 2–3 genuinely well-designed photographer portfolio sites first and form a point of view; then decide the layout, composition, and section design yourself. Do not fall back to a generic centered-hero + three-identical-cards template — bring editorial composition, asymmetry, scale contrast, and rhythm. **Vary your hero composition to fit this brand specifically — no headline over the photograph, no buttons in the first screen, no icon anywhere on the page; the photographs are the argument and the type stays out of their way.** The sections below define **what the page must communicate, not how it must look.**

## Style guide (constraints, not layout)

**Palette — flat colors only, no gradients:**

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Surface, light | `#F5F2EC` (bone) |
| Surface, dark bands | `#111111` |
| Ink / headings | `#111111` |
| Accent — hairlines, eyebrows, and the single inquiry link only | `#C9A9A6` (dusty rose) |
| Muted body text | `#7B7773` |
| Hairlines / dividers | `#E3DED5` |

**Typography:** Display = **Cormorant** (fallback: Playfair Display), light italic for headlines, set large with generous leading. Body = **Jost**, 16px, line-height ~1.8; labels and the navigation in uppercase Jost with 0.14em tracking at 11–12px.

**Feel:** silence — whitespace as the main material, photographs given the full width, type small and precise. No shadows, no rounded corners, no icons, no coloured buttons: actions are text links with a hairline underline.

**Motion:** a slow crossfade on images as they enter; nothing else. Use only what the chosen builder provides natively; **no custom JavaScript and no hand-rolled motion CSS.**

## Design signature (make this page distinct — adapt as you see fit)
These are compositional cues, not layout mechanics. Use them to give this photographer her own look rather than a generic template:
- **Hero:** one photograph, edge to edge, taller than the viewport is wide; the name set in 11px uppercase Jost at the top-left corner; the italic headline appears **below** the photograph on bone, with one sentence beside it, and no call to action until the gallery has been seen.
- **Gallery:** a three-column masonry of nine photographs with uneven heights and 24px gutters; no captions, no hover overlays, no lightbox chrome beyond what the builder does natively.
- **Collections:** pricing as one table — three columns, rows for hours, second shooter, engagement session, album, delivery time, price — with hairlines and no "most popular" badge.
- **Shape language:** square corners; hairlines in dusty rose; one dark band for the two quotes, set very large in Cormorant italic.

## Sections — what each must communicate (design them your way)

1. **Hero** — restraint and confidence: the name, one photograph, an italic headline below it, one sentence on where she works.
2. **Gallery** — nine photographs that share one grade: warm, film-like, natural light; curate for one couple's day, not nine random weddings.
3. **Approach** — three short paragraphs under small uppercase labels (Before · The day · After): how she plans, how she works unobtrusively, what couples receive and when.
4. **Collections** — the three collections below as a single table; state what every collection includes.
5. **Kind words** — two quotes only, set very large on the dark band, with couples' first names and the venue; write them yourself.
6. **Details** — the facts below on travel, availability, and how many weddings she takes a year.
7. **Inquire** — a working inquiry form (your names, wedding date, venue or city, email, guest count, how you found her, a few words about the day) built with the builder's native form element. If the builder has no form element, use a clearly labelled email call-to-action instead of omitting this.
8. **Footer** — name, based in, email, Instagram handle, copyright.

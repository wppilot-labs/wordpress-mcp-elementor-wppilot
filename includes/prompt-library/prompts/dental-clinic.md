# Bright Harbor Dental — family dental clinic landing page

Design and build a **complete, production-quality landing page** for "Bright Harbor Dental", a family dental practice. **You own the design.** If web search is available, research 2–3 genuinely well-designed dental or clinic sites first and form a point of view; then decide the layout, composition, and section design yourself. Do not fall back to a generic centered-hero + three-identical-cards template — bring editorial composition, asymmetry, scale contrast, and rhythm. **Vary your hero composition to fit this brand specifically — no smiling-stock-model hero, no photo behind the headline; the first screen should let an anxious person act immediately, so the appointment request belongs in the hero itself.** The sections below define **what the page must communicate, not how it must look.**

## Style guide (constraints, not layout)

**Palette — flat colors only, no gradients:**

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Surface, light | `#FFFFFF` |
| Surface, tinted panels | `#EAF3F8` (pale sky) |
| Surface, dark bands | `#0F2A44` (navy) |
| Ink / headings | `#0F2A44` |
| Accent — use sparingly: prices, buttons, the emergency line, numerals | `#1FA8A0` (aqua) |
| Muted body text | `#5B6B77` |
| Hairlines / dividers | `#D8E3EA` |

**Typography:** Display = **Manrope** (fallback: Inter), weight 800, tight letter-spacing (−0.02em). Body = **Manrope**, 16–18px, line-height ~1.7. One typeface family throughout, so the page feels exact rather than decorated.

**Feel:** calm, clinical, orderly — pale tints, plenty of white, soft 8px corners on panels (consistently, everywhere), no shadows, no gradients, no illustrations. Photography only in one full-width band and the team section, graded bright and neutral.

**Motion:** minimal — a gentle fade on section entry. Use only what the chosen builder provides natively; **no custom JavaScript and no hand-rolled motion CSS.**

## Design signature (make this page distinct — adapt as you see fit)
These are compositional cues, not layout mechanics. Use them to give this clinic its own look rather than a generic template:
- **Hero:** two columns on white — left, the headline, a reassuring sentence, and a phone number in aqua; right, the appointment-request form itself on a pale-sky panel with 8px corners. No image in the hero; the first photograph is the full-width band that follows.
- **Services:** a 2×3 grid of rows, each a hairline-ruled panel with the treatment name, one sentence on what to expect, a duration, and a "from" price in aqua numerals — no icons, no marketing cards.
- **New patient visit:** four numbered steps set as a single horizontal line on the navy band, numerals oversized in aqua.
- **Shape language:** 8px corners everywhere, hairlines over shadows, one navy band, aqua reserved for numbers and actions.

## Sections — what each must communicate (design them your way)

1. **Hero** — a trustworthy, calm practice: the brand name, a headline, one reassuring sentence, the phone number, and a working appointment-request form (full name, phone, email, new or existing patient, reason for visit, preferred day) built with the builder's native form element. If the builder has no form element, use a clearly labelled call/email call-to-action instead of omitting this.
2. **Reassurance band** — one full-width photograph of the treatment room with a single line over a navy plate: gentle care for nervous patients, sedation available.
3. **Proof bar** — four facts: New patients seen within 48 hours · 4.9 average from 620 reviews · Same-day emergency care · All major insurance accepted.
4. **Services** — all six services below with what to expect, duration, and starting price.
5. **Your first visit** — four steps: a 10-minute conversation, digital X-rays, a full exam, a written plan with prices before anything is done.
6. **Insurance & pricing** — the plans accepted, the in-house membership plan below, and the promise that estimates are in writing before treatment.
7. **Team** — three dentists with a photo, qualification, and one line on how they practise.
8. **Reviews** — three distinct, believable patient quotes with first names; write them yourself.
9. **Visit & emergencies** — address, hours per day, phone, email, the emergency line, and a map if the builder supports one.
10. **Footer** — brand + tagline, quick links, contact, copyright.

# Wagford Grooming Co. — dog grooming salon landing page

Design and build a **complete, production-quality landing page** for "Wagford Grooming Co.", a neighbourhood dog grooming salon. **You own the design.** If web search is available, research 2–3 genuinely well-designed pet-care or grooming sites first and form a point of view; then decide the layout, composition, and section design yourself. Do not fall back to a generic centered-hero + three-identical-cards template — bring editorial composition, asymmetry, scale contrast, and rhythm. **Vary your hero composition to fit this brand specifically — no paw-print pattern, no wavy section dividers, no photo collage; the hero is a hard vertical split of two flat colour blocks with one dog photograph inside a circle, and the page earns its playfulness through colour and rounded shapes, not decoration.** The sections below define **what the page must communicate, not how it must look.**

## Style guide (constraints, not layout)

**Palette — flat colors only, no gradients:**

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Surface, light | `#FFF3D6` (butter) |
| Surface, white panels | `#FFFFFF` |
| Ink / headings | `#1E1A2B` (plum ink) |
| Accent, primary — hero block, buttons, prices | `#FF6F61` (coral) |
| Accent, secondary — tags, the segmented control's active state, icons | `#1F9E89` (teal) |
| Muted body text | `#6B6479` |
| Hairlines / dividers | `#EADFC8` |

**Typography:** Display = **Nunito** (fallback: Quicksand), weight 900 — round, friendly, set large. Body = **Nunito**, 16–18px, line-height ~1.7. Prices in Nunito 900.

**Feel:** cheerful and clean — this is the one page in the set where rounded corners are the rule: 24px on cards and panels, pill buttons, circular photo masks. Two accents instead of one because the brand is playful, but still flat colour and still no gradients or shadows. Photography of real dogs mid-groom, bright and warm.

**Motion:** bouncy but brief — a small scale-in on cards as they enter, and nothing that loops. Use only what the chosen builder provides natively; **no custom JavaScript and no hand-rolled motion CSS.**

## Design signature (make this page distinct — adapt as you see fit)
These are compositional cues, not layout mechanics. Use them to give this salon its own look rather than a generic template:
- **Hero:** a straight vertical split — the left half butter with the headline, one sentence, and a pill "Book a groom" button; the right half solid coral holding a single photograph of a freshly groomed dog inside a large circular mask. No text on the coral side.
- **Pricing by size:** a segmented control (Small / Medium / Large / XL) built with the builder's native tabs, switching one clean price table; teal marks the active segment.
- **Services:** six rounded white cards on butter, each with a teal SVG icon, the service name, one line, and "from" pricing in coral.
- **Shape language:** 24px corners, pills, circles; hairlines only inside tables; coral for actions and prices, teal for tags and icons.

## Sections — what each must communicate (design them your way)

1. **Hero** — a salon that dogs and their owners like: the brand name, a headline, one sentence, the booking action, and the hours today.
2. **Proof bar** — four facts: Fear Free certified groomers · 2,400 pups groomed a year · Open 7 days · Est. 2016.
3. **Services** — all six services below with one line each and starting prices.
4. **Pricing by size** — the full price table below, switchable by dog size.
5. **Add-ons** — the add-on list below as a compact two-column list with prices.
6. **Meet the groomers** — three groomers with a circular photo, one line on experience, and their favourite breed to groom.
7. **Before & after** — four pairs of photographs in rounded frames with the dog's name and breed; write the names yourself.
8. **Reviews** — three distinct, believable owner quotes with the owner's first name and the dog's name; write them yourself.
9. **Book** — a working booking form (owner name, phone, email, dog's name, breed, size, service, preferred date, anything we should know) built with the builder's native form element, with the phone number given equal prominence. If the builder has no form element, use a clearly labelled call/email call-to-action instead of omitting this.
10. **Visit** — address, hours per day, phone, email, parking, and a map if the builder supports one.
11. **Footer** — brand + tagline, quick links, contact, copyright.

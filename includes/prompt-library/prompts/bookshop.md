# Lantern & Quill Books — independent bookshop landing page

Design and build a **complete, production-quality landing page** for "Lantern & Quill Books", an independent bookshop. **You own the design.** If web search is available, research 2–3 genuinely well-designed bookshop or publisher sites first and form a point of view; then decide the layout, composition, and section design yourself. Do not fall back to a generic centered-hero + three-identical-cards template — bring editorial composition, asymmetry, scale contrast, and rhythm. **Vary your hero composition to fit this brand specifically — no photograph in the hero, no shelves-of-books stock image anywhere above the fold; the opener is typographic, like the title page of a well-made book, and the first images on the page are book covers.** The sections below define **what the page must communicate, not how it must look.**

## Style guide (constraints, not layout)

**Palette — flat colors only, no gradients:**

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Surface, light | `#F8F5EE` (paper) |
| Surface, dark bands | `#161616` (ink) |
| Ink / headings | `#161616` |
| Accent — use sparingly: one word, prices, the event dates, links | `#7A1F2B` (oxblood) |
| Muted body text | `#6E6A63` |
| Hairlines / dividers | `#DAD5CA` |

**Typography:** Display = **Libre Caslon Text** (fallback: EB Garamond) — use true small caps for the shop name and section titles, italics for epigraphs and book titles. Body = **Libre Franklin**, 16–18px, line-height ~1.75. Prices and dates in Libre Caslon with old-style figures if available.

**Feel:** a printed book — centred title page, hairline frames, an ornament rule (a single centred hairline with a small oxblood diamond) between sections, drop caps on the first paragraph of the about text. No shadows, no rounded corners, no icons above the fold.

**Motion:** none beyond a soft fade on section entry. Use only what the chosen builder provides natively; **no custom JavaScript and no hand-rolled motion CSS.**

## Design signature (make this page distinct — adapt as you see fit)
These are compositional cues, not layout mechanics. Use them to give this shop its own look rather than a generic template:
- **Hero:** a centred title page inside a single hairline frame: a tiny uppercase eyebrow ("Booksellers since 2009"), the shop name in small caps at large size, an italic two-line epigraph as the headline, and the address and hours set as a colophon line at the foot of the frame. One text link beneath the frame: "This month's picks".
- **Staff picks:** six book covers in a 3×2 grid, each with title in italics, author, price in oxblood, and one sentence from a named bookseller — covers are the only images on the upper page.
- **Events:** a dated ledger — a left column of dates in Caslon, a right column with title, guest, and time, separated by hairlines; no event cards.
- **Shape language:** square corners, hairline frames and ornament rules, one ink band for the book clubs, oxblood reserved for prices, dates and links.

## Sections — what each must communicate (design them your way)

1. **Hero** — the sense of a shop with a point of view: the name, an epigraph-style headline, the hours today, one link to the picks.
2. **Proof line** — four facts set as a single centred line with ornaments: Independent since 2009 · 14,000 titles in stock · Special orders in 2–4 days · Free gift wrapping.
3. **This month's picks** — the six books below with author, price, and a one-sentence bookseller note; write the notes yourself in distinct voices.
4. **Events** — the five events below as a dated ledger, each with a "reserve a seat" link.
5. **Book clubs** — the three clubs below on the ink band: name, what they read, when they meet, how to join.
6. **About** — one drop-capped paragraph on the shop and the two owners; write it yourself, factual and warm.
7. **Special orders** — the promise (any book in print, 2–4 days, no deposit) and a working order form (title, author, your name, email, phone, notes) built with the builder's native form element. If the builder has no form element, use a clearly labelled call/email call-to-action instead of omitting this.
8. **Reviews** — three distinct, believable customer quotes with first names; write them yourself.
9. **Visit** — address, hours per day, phone, email, and a map if the builder supports one.
10. **Newsletter** — one line, one email field: new arrivals and events every Thursday.
11. **Footer** — brand + tagline, quick links, contact, copyright.

# Ironbark Brewing Co. — craft brewery taproom landing page

Design and build a **complete, production-quality landing page** for "Ironbark Brewing Co.", a craft brewery with a taproom. **You own the design.** If web search is available, research 2–3 genuinely well-designed brewery or taproom sites first and form a point of view; then decide the layout, composition, and section design yourself. Do not fall back to a generic centered-hero + three-identical-cards template — bring editorial composition, asymmetry, scale contrast, and rhythm. **Vary your hero composition to fit this brand specifically — no full-screen photo of a pint, no beer-can lineup, no hop illustrations; the hero is the tap list itself, set like a chalkboard menu in type, and the first photograph arrives only after it.** The sections below define **what the page must communicate, not how it must look.**

## Style guide (constraints, not layout)

**Palette — flat colors only, no gradients:**

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Surface, dark (the page's default ground) | `#121212` |
| Surface, light bands | `#EFE9DD` (bone) |
| Ink on light | `#121212` |
| Text on dark | `#EFE9DD` |
| Accent — tap numbers, ABV, prices, buttons | `#D98E04` (amber) |
| Muted text on dark | `#9A948A` |
| Hairlines on dark | `#2A2A2A` |

**Typography:** Display = **Bebas Neue** (fallback: Anton) — tall, uppercase, set very large for the wordmark and section titles. Body = **IBM Plex Sans**, 16–18px, line-height ~1.7. Tap list columns (style, ABV, IBU, price) in **IBM Plex Mono**, tabular, so the ledger lines up.

**Feel:** dark, industrial, taproom-at-night — steel, wood, and amber light. Square corners; hairlines in `#2A2A2A`; no shadows; no gradients; the page is dark by default with two bone-coloured bands for contrast.

**Motion:** a subtle stagger on the tap-list rows as they enter; nothing else. Use only what the chosen builder provides natively; **no custom JavaScript and no hand-rolled motion CSS.**

## Design signature (make this page distinct — adapt as you see fit)
These are compositional cues, not layout mechanics. Use them to give this brewery its own look rather than a generic template:
- **Hero:** the wordmark small at top-left, then "ON TAP THIS WEEK" in Bebas at the largest size on the page, then a numbered ledger of eight beers — number in amber, name in Bebas, style / ABV / IBU / price in Plex Mono columns with hairlines between rows; two actions beneath (Visit the taproom / Book a private event).
- **Photo strip:** one full-width photograph of the taproom, cropped to a short band (about a third of the viewport tall), directly under the ledger — the first and largest image on the page.
- **This week:** a horizontal calendar strip, seven columns for the days, each with the food truck and any event; today's column marked in amber.
- **Shape language:** square everything; hairlines; two bone bands (story and private events); amber reserved for numerals, prices and buttons.

## Sections — what each must communicate (design them your way)

1. **Hero** — a brewery that is pouring right now: the wordmark, the full tap list below with every column, and two actions.
2. **Photo strip** — the room, at night, full of people.
3. **Proof bar** — four facts: Brewed on site since 2018 · 12 taps, 8 rotating · Dog-friendly patio · Growlers and crowlers to go.
4. **This week at the taproom** — the food-truck and event calendar below as a seven-day strip.
5. **The brewery** — one short story paragraph on the bone band: who founded it, the 10-barrel system, the flagship beer; write it from the facts below.
6. **Beer to go** — growler, crowler and 4-pack pricing.
7. **Private events** — the taproom's private-hire offer (the mezzanine for up to 40, the whole room for up to 120) and a working enquiry form (name, email, phone, date, group size, occasion) built with the builder's native form element. If the builder has no form element, use a clearly labelled email call-to-action instead of omitting this.
8. **Reviews** — three distinct, believable visitor quotes with first names; write them yourself.
9. **Visit** — address, hours per day, phone, email, parking, and a map if the builder supports one.
10. **Footer** — wordmark, quick links, contact, copyright, and the line "Please drink responsibly. 21+ to purchase."
